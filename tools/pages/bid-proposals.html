How a proposal job works

  1. Send the solicitation. Include any amendments and your deadline.
  2. Get a fixed quote. It arrives within one business day, with a short questionnaire about your company, people and past work.
  3. Pay to start. Proposals of $500 or more take a 50% deposit, with the balance due within 7 days of delivery.
  4. Receive your draft. It is checked against every requirement in the solicitation before it reaches you.
  5. Revise and submit. Two rounds of revisions are included. You review the final version and submit it yourself.

Turnaround is counted in business days from when we have the solicitation and your questionnaire answers.

What's not included

We don't set your prices (you supply the figures), give legal advice, or submit bids on your behalf. No one can guarantee a contract award.
