What you get

  • A one-page capability statement written for government and corporate buyers, delivered as a PDF and as an editable Word file.
  • Rewritten company description text you can paste into your SBA small business profile.
  • Two rounds of revisions. Reply to the delivery email with your changes.

Written, not just designed. Many low-cost capability statement services are design jobs: you supply the words and they lay them out. We write the content for you, from your questionnaire answers and public records about your company, and then lay out the page.

What goes on it

  • Company overview. Who you are and who you serve, in two or three sentences.
  • Core competencies. The work you do, described in the terms buyers use.
  • Differentiators. The concrete reasons a buyer should choose you over the next bidder.
  • Past performance. Recent, relevant jobs with the client, scope, dates and results.
  • Company data. UEI, CAGE code, NAICS codes, certifications, insurance and bonding, and service area.
  • Contact. The person buyers should call or email.

How it works

  1. Order online. Pay $300 securely through Stripe.
  2. Answer a short questionnaire. It arrives by email within one business day and asks about your company, your people and your past work.

What we'll ask you for

  • Your legal business name, address, years in business and number of employees.
  • Your UEI, CAGE code and NAICS codes, if you have them.
  • Certifications (such as 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB or SDVOSB), licenses, bonding and insurance.
  • Three recent jobs: the client, the scope, the dates and how it went.
  • What you want to be known for and the buyers you want to reach.
  • Your logo and any past materials, if you have them.

Questions

Will you add anything I haven't done?

No. We write only from what you tell us and from public records about your company, such as your SBA profile or website. If something is missing, we ask you instead of guessing.

Do I need to be registered in SAM.gov first?

No, you can order at any stage. If you are registered, we include your UEI and CAGE code, which federal buyers look for. Our guide to finding and bidding on government contracts in Mississippi covers registration.

Can you do it faster?

Yes, when our schedule allows. Rush delivery in 1 business day costs 50% more ($450). Ask before you order and we'll confirm the date.

Can I update it later?

Yes. You own the finished document, and the Word file lets you change it as your company grows.

How is this different from a proposal?

A capability statement introduces your company to any buyer. A proposal answers one specific solicitation. If you have a bid due, see bid proposal writing and reviews.
