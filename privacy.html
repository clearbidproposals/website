Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Clearbid Proposal Services ("Clearbid," "we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Clearbid is owned by Kaleb Hill and based in Petal, Mississippi. Questions about this policy can be sent to kaleb@clearbidproposals.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you request a quote or order a service, you send us information such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, solicitations, company history, past performance, staff details, certifications and pricing.
  • Payment information. Payments are handled by Stripe. We see your name, email address, the amount paid and the last four digits of your card, but not your full card or bank account number.
  • Public business information. To find businesses that may need our services, we review public sources such as government contractor registrations, small business directories and company websites. We collect only business contact details published there, such as a business name and a published business email address.

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and revise the work you order, and to communicate with you about it.
  • To process payments, send receipts and invoices, and keep business and tax records.
  • To send a small number of business emails introducing our services to businesses whose contact details are published publicly.

Service providers we use

We share information only with the providers we need to run the business, and only as needed for them to provide their service:

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Google Workspace provides our email and file storage.
  • AI-assisted research and drafting tools help prepare your documents. Your materials are sent to these tools only to prepare your work.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, and Google Fonts supplies its typefaces. Like most web services, they may log your IP address and browser details when you visit.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not share your documents, pricing or company information with other clients.
  • This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics.

Business emails from us

If we emailed you about our services, we found your business address in a public listing. Every such email identifies us and includes our mailing address. Reply "no thanks" (or anything like "remove" or "unsubscribe") and we will not email you again.

How long we keep information

We keep project files while they are useful for revisions and future work you may request. Payment and invoice records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask us to delete your project files at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing kaleb@clearbidproposals.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may keep records we are legally required to retain.

Security

We use established providers and take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
