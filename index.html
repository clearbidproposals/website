Section 2

How it works

  1. Send the solicitation. Email the RFP, bid packet, or opportunity link with your deadline.
  2. Confirm scope and price. You receive a fixed quote and a short intake questionnaire about your company, people, and past work.
  3. Pay to start. Services under $500 are paid upfront; larger proposals take a 50% deposit, with the balance due within 7 days of delivery.
  4. Receive your draft. A complete draft arrives by email, built with an AI-assisted research and drafting process and checked against every requirement in the solicitation before delivery.
  5. Revise and submit. Two rounds of revisions are included. You review the final version and submit it yourself.

Section 4

Payment and policies

Payment

Pay online by card or bank transfer through Stripe. Services under $500 are paid upfront. Proposals of $500 or more take a 50% deposit to start, with the balance due within 7 days of delivery. Monthly support is charged automatically each month; cancel anytime, effective at the end of the paid month.

Refunds

Cancel before work begins for a full refund. After drafting starts, the deposit covers work completed. If a draft misses the agreed scope, we revise it or refund the balance.

What's not included

Pricing figures (you set your price), legal review, and submission on your behalf. No one can guarantee a contract award.

Confidentiality

Your documents and pricing are used only to prepare your proposal and are never shared with other clients.
