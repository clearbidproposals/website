Section 3

How it works

  1. Send the solicitation. Email the RFP, bid packet, or opportunity link with your deadline.
  2. Confirm scope and price. You receive a fixed quote and a short intake questionnaire about your company, people, and past work.
  3. Pay to start. Services under $500 are paid upfront; larger proposals take a 50% deposit, with the balance due within 7 days of delivery.
  4. Receive your draft. A complete draft arrives by email, built with an AI-assisted research and drafting process and checked against every requirement in the solicitation before delivery.
  5. Revise and submit. Two rounds of revisions are included. You review the final version and submit it yourself.

Payment and policies

Payment

Pay online by card or bank transfer through Stripe. Services under $500 are paid upfront. Proposals of $500 or more take a 50% deposit to start, with the balance due within 7 days of delivery. Your documents and pricing are used only to prepare your proposal and are never shared with other clients.

Section 6

Frequently asked questions

Do you submit the bid for me?

No. You review the final version and submit it through the buyer's portal, email or mail. That keeps your signature, certifications and pricing in your hands.

Do you set our pricing?

No. You supply the price or rate sheet, and we write the proposal around it. We never change your numbers.

What do you need from us to start?

The full solicitation and any amendments, your deadline, and answers to a short intake questionnaire about your company, key people and past work. Your UEI, CAGE code and certifications help if you have them.

How fast can you turn it around?

Bid reviews in 2 business days, capability statements in 3, standard proposals in 5, counted from when we have the solicitation and your intake answers. If your deadline is tight, email us before you pay and we'll tell you honestly whether we can meet it.

Do you use AI?

Yes. We use an AI-assisted research and drafting process, which is how we keep turnaround fast and prices fixed. Every document is built only from the information you give us and public records about your company, and checked against each requirement in the solicitation before delivery.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Your documents and pricing are used only to prepare your work and are never shared with or reused for other clients. See the privacy policy for details.

What if I need changes?

Two rounds of revisions are included with every service. Reply to the delivery email with your changes and you'll get an updated version, usually within one to two business days.

Can you guarantee we'll win?

No one honestly can. We make sure your proposal is complete, compliant and clear, which removes the most common reasons bids are scored down or rejected.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer through Stripe. Capability statements, bid reviews and monthly support can be ordered with the buttons above; proposals are invoiced after you approve the quote.
